Ada Lovelace

A tribute page

About Ada Lovelace

Ada Lovelace was born in 1815 and died in 1852. She worked with Charles Babbage on the Analytical Engine. In her notes, she described a method for calculating Bernoulli numbers, which is often considered the first computer program. Her work made her an important figure in the history of computing.

Important events

  1. 1815 - Ada Lovelace was born in London.
  2. 1833 - She met Charles Babbage.
  3. 1843 - She published notes about the Analytical Engine.
  4. 1852 - Ada Lovelace died in London.

Contributions

  • Notes about the Analytical Engine
  • An algorithm for Bernoulli numbers
  • Early ideas about general-purpose computers
